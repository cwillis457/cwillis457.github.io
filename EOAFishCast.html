FEATURES

Everything on the water. Nothing in the cloud.

EOA Fish Cast keeps a complete record of every outing — catches, spots, conditions, and photos — stored privately on your iPhone.

🐟

Catch Log

Log every catch with species, date, time, weight, length, water body, bait or lure, water conditions, and personal notes. Your full fishing history, always on device.

📍

Fishing Spot Saver

Drop a GPS pin on any spot — dock, cove, channel break, or that exact spot off the bank where you know they're holding. Name it, add notes, find it again.

📸

Photo Log PRO

Attach a photo to every catch and every saved spot. Your biggest bass, favorite lure, best morning light — stored permanently in the app alongside the data.

🏆

Personal Records

Automatic personal bests by species — biggest ever, longest ever, most caught in a day. The app tracks records as you log, so you always know when you've topped yourself.

📊

Trip Analytics PRO

Review your best days by water body, species, season, bait, and conditions. Spot patterns. Know which spots produce in which month and what conditions set off the bite.

📋

CSV Export PRO

Export your full catch log as a CSV file — share with a fishing partner, open in Numbers or Excel, or back up your records any time you want.

🌤

Conditions Tracking

Log water temperature, clarity, wind direction, sky cover, and moon phase at the time of each catch. Over time, build a real picture of what conditions trigger bites.

🎣

Multi-Species Support

Bass, crappie, bluegill, catfish, trout, walleye, pike, redfish, flounder — any species you log. Custom species names supported so any fish you catch fits your log.

📵

Works Completely Offline

No internet required on the water. GPS works without cell service. All logging, spot saving, and photo storage happens on your phone — no signal, no problem.

HOW IT WORKS

Log in seconds. Review for seasons.

Designed to be fast enough to use the moment you land a fish — before you even unhook it.

01

Land the fish

Tap New Catch — species, weight, and location auto-fill from your last entry so repeat catches take five seconds to log.

02

Snap a photo (Pro)

Attach a photo while the fish is still in hand. It saves with the catch data so every record is a real moment, not just numbers.

03

Pin the spot

One tap saves your current GPS location as a named spot. Find the exact point again — not just the general area.

04

Review your patterns

Over time, your log becomes a real fishing intelligence tool — what works where, when, and under what conditions.

PRIVACY

Your catches stay your catches.

EOA Fish Cast stores everything on your iPhone. No account. No server. No analytics. Your fishing spots and personal records are yours alone.

✓

No account required

EOA Fish Cast requires no sign-up, no email, and no account of any kind — before or after the Pro upgrade.

✓

All data stored on-device

Every catch, spot, photo, and condition log is stored in the app's private folder on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

✓

GPS is local only

Location is used to tag catches and save spots, stored on your device. Your coordinates are never transmitted anywhere.

✓

Photos add-only permission

If you choose to save a catch photo to your library, the app requests add-only permission — it cannot read or browse your existing photos.

✓

No analytics or tracking

EOA Fish Cast contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

✓

Works fully offline

No internet connection is needed at any point. GPS, logging, photo storage, and all Pro features work without cell service or Wi-Fi.
