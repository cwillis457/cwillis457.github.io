Support

Need help?

Common questions and how to get in touch.

Bluetooth not working

Make sure Bluetooth is enabled in Settings. On iOS 16 and later, you may also need to enable Developer Mode under Settings → Privacy & Security. Both devices must have Bluetooth on and be within range.

NFC not responding

NFC requires iPhone 7 or later. Unlock your iPhone before tapping — NFC is disabled on the lock screen. Hold the top of your iPhone near the other device's NFC area (usually the back, center).

Card scan missed some fields

Lay the card flat on a dark, contrasting surface in good light. After scanning, all fields are editable in the review screen before saving — just correct anything the scan missed.

Contact not saving

Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Contacts and make sure EOA Biz Card has permission. If it was denied, tap the toggle to allow access, then try saving again.

Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device.

EOA Biz Card does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. Everything lives on your iPhone.

✓

No server, no account

Your business card details are stored only in your iPhone's local storage. No account is required and no sign-in is needed.

✓

Bluetooth data stays local

When you share via Bluetooth, your card is transmitted directly device-to-device. No data passes through any server or third party.

✓

Camera used only on request

The camera is only accessed when you actively tap Scan Card. Images are processed entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework and are never saved or uploaded.

✓

Contacts permission is optional

Contacts access is only requested when you choose to save a received card. The app reads nothing from your existing contacts.

✓

No analytics or tracking

EOA Biz Card contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

✓

NFC writes only

NFC is used only to write your card to another device. The app never reads NFC tags from the environment.

Last updated: June 2026. This policy applies to EOA Biz Card for iOS, version 1.0 and later. EOA Biz Card does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data with third parties. No data retention policy is needed because no data is collected. For questions, contact WillisStrategy@outlook.com.
