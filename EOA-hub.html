§ 02 — Architecture

What "On Device" Actually Means

Privacy claims are cheap. These are the specific engineering decisions behind them, and they are the same in every EOA title.

01 / STORAGE

There is no server

Willis Strategy Group does not operate a backend for these apps. There is no database holding your contacts, mail, ledger, routes, or scores — because no copy exists anywhere but your phone.

02 / TRACKING

Nothing is measured

No analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier. Nobody counts what you tap, and there is no usage record to sell, breach, or subpoena.

03 / CREDENTIALS

Keychain only

When an app needs a password or an OAuth token, it goes into Apple's encrypted Keychain on your device. It is never transmitted to us, because there is nowhere to transmit it.

04 / DEPENDENCIES

System frameworks only

Every EOA app is built against Apple's own frameworks — Network, AVFoundation, CryptoKit, PhotosUI, WebKit. No third-party SDKs ship in the binary, so nothing arrives that we did not write.
