EOA Golf Pro

Three ways to find the pin.

No licensed course-map database behind any of these — just three different methods that check each other instead of asking you to trust one number blindly. GPS is free; the satellite and camera methods come with EOA Golf Pro, which also unlocks scoring for up to four players.

GPS — free

Walk it once

Walk to the flag, tap Mark Pin Here. Every round after that, get live GPS distance from wherever you're standing. Expect roughly ±5–15 yds of normal phone GPS drift. Free, always.

Satellite — Pro

Drop a pin from above

Never played the hole? Tap the green on a satellite photo instead. Shows distance to roughly the middle of the green, not the day's exact pin position.

Camera — Pro

Photograph the flagstick

Tap the top and base of the flag in a photo. Estimates distance from its apparent height — depends on an assumed flagstick height and how level the phone was held.

When more than one source exists for a hole, the app cross-references them — showing how closely a GPS pin and a satellite pin agree, or how a camera estimate compares to whichever pin is already saved. A close match is reassuring; a real gap gets flagged rather than silently resolved.

Privacy & Legal

Privacy Policy & Legal Notices

Last updated: July 4, 2026. This page covers EOA Golf Score on iOS. It is written to be accurate to how the app currently works — it should be reviewed and re-published whenever the app's data handling or course list changes.

The short version: EOA Golf Score doesn't have user accounts, doesn't run analytics or ad tracking, and doesn't send your data to a server we operate. Everything you enter — scorecards, rounds, scores, notes, GPS pins, photos you scan — stays on your device. The only outgoing network call the app makes is a brief, anonymous address lookup (via Apple's MapKit) when you first open the satellite map picker for a course, so the map can center on the right place.

What we collect

EOA Golf Score does not require an account, email address, or sign-in of any kind. The app does not collect your name, contact information, or any data that identifies you to us — because nothing you do in the app is transmitted to a server we control.

Information you create in the app — scorecards you add or edit, rounds you play and save, scores and notes per hole, GPS and map pin locations, and photos you take for scorecard scanning or the camera range finder — is stored locally in the app's own storage on your device. We have no copy of it and no way to access it.

Location & camera access

The app requests two permissions, each used only for the specific feature that needs it:

  • Location ("When In Use") — used by the GPS range finder to read your current position and calculate live distance to a pin you've marked, and briefly by the satellite map picker as a fallback location if a course's address can't be geocoded. Location data is used in the moment it's needed and is not logged, stored in a history, or sent anywhere by us.
  • Camera — used for two features: scanning a photo of a paper scorecard to help fill in a new course, and the camera range finder's flagstick-distance estimate. Photos taken for these features are processed entirely on-device (Apple's Vision framework for text recognition; on-device geometry for the distance estimate) and are not uploaded anywhere.

You can review or revoke either permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. Declining a permission only disables the feature that needs it — the rest of the app continues to work normally.

Where your data lives

All app data is stored locally using standard iOS on-device storage. It is included in your device's normal iOS backups (iCloud or computer backup, depending on your settings) the same way any app's local data would be, but EOA Golf Score itself does not sync, back up, or transmit this data to any external service. Deleting the app deletes this data permanently, with no copy retained anywhere by us.

Third-party services

The app uses two of Apple's own system frameworks that involve brief network activity, both fully covered by Apple's own privacy practices rather than a separate third party:

  • MapKit geocoding — when you open the satellite map pin picker for a course, the app sends that course's street address to Apple's geocoding service to center the map. No personal or account information is included in this request.
  • App Store / StoreKit — standard Apple infrastructure for app distribution and the one-time EOA Golf Pro purchase. Apple handles the transaction; the app receives only a confirmation that the purchase is entitled, never your payment details. No purchase is required to use the app's free features.

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. No data broker, ad network, or analytics company receives any information from this app.

Accuracy of scorecards, distances, and estimates

Course information in this app — par, yardage, handicap index, course rating, and slope — comes from a mix of official sources, public listings, and in some cases estimates, as disclosed individually for each built-in course inside the app. None of it is guaranteed accurate or current, and none of it is a substitute for a course's own official scorecard, especially for anything involving an official handicap or tournament play. Every value is editable so you can correct it from your own experience.

None of the three range finder methods (GPS, satellite map, or camera) provide survey-grade accuracy:

  • GPS distances rely on your device's location hardware and typically carry roughly ±5–15 yards of error depending on sky visibility and tree cover.
  • Satellite map pins show distance to where a pin was tapped on aerial imagery, which reflects the general area of the green — not the course's actual daily pin placement, which moves and which this app has no way to know.
  • Camera estimates depend on an assumed flagstick height (user-adjustable, defaulting to 7 feet) and the device's tilt at the moment of capture, both of which are approximations, not certainties.

Use these tools as planning aids, not as a substitute for your own judgment or for an official, certified rangefinder where the rules of an event require one.

Course affiliation

EOA Golf Score is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any golf course named or featured in this app, including Maple Ridge Golf Club, Bull Creek Golf Course (East and West), Oxbow Creek Golf Course, Godwin Creek Golf Course, or Lakewood Golf Course. Course names are used solely to identify which real-world course a given scorecard corresponds to. Any course names, logos, or marks remain the property of their respective owners.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information from any user — local-only storage, no accounts, no analytics — this risk is structurally limited regardless of a user's age.

Changes to this policy

If how the app handles data changes in a future version — for example, if a future purchase feature or cloud sync is introduced — this page will be updated accordingly, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect that change. Material changes affecting how personal information is handled would also be reflected in the App Store listing's privacy disclosures at the time of that update.
